🧪 Brain Tricks • Trick it to do hard tasks

TRICK 1 • 2-MIN RULE

Make It Too Small To Fail

Brain fears "Study 2 hours". It doesn't fear "Open book for 2 min". Start with 2 min, momentum does rest. This is Activation Energy trick.

TRICK 2 • TEMPTATION BUNDLING

Pair Pain + Pleasure

Only listen to favourite music/podcast WHILE doing hard task. Brain starts craving hard task for reward.

Example: While I study, I get to drink cold coffee.

TRICK 3 • DOPAMINE DETOX

Earn Dopamine, Don't Steal It

Morning: NO reels, NO sugar, NO porn. First dopamine must come from WORK. If you steal dopamine early, brain won't work all day.

✅ Allowed before work: Water, sunlight, walk, cold shower
❌ Not allowed: Instagram, YouTube, Gaming

TRICK 4 • IF-THEN PLAN

Pre-Decide, Don't Decide

Willpower fails when you decide in the moment. Pre-decide:

"IF I feel like scrolling, THEN I will stand up and drink water."

TRICK 5 • FUTURE SELF

Talk To Future You

Close eyes 30 sec. Imagine you 90 days from now - disciplined, fit, calm. What would he tell you now? He would say: "Don't quit today."

TRICK 6 • 5-SECOND RULE

5-4-3-2-1 GO

When brain says "later", count 5-4-3-2-1 and MOVE your body. Movement breaks overthinking. Mel Robbins rule.
